Data Deletion

Data Deletion

Last updated: August 11, 2026

These instructions explain how to request deletion of personal data processed by Stark Paid Media, operated by 21Shift SpA under the 21Shift brand in Chile.

Submit a Deletion Request

Email info@21shift.com with the subject line Stark Paid Media Data Deletion Request. We will review requests concerning personal data processed in connection with authorized paid-media analysis and reporting.

Do not send passwords or access tokens.

What to Include

Please identify the relevant person, organization, Page, or ad account, and explain your relationship to it. Include enough detail for us to locate the relevant information, such as the organization name, Page name, or ad account name. Do not include credentials, passwords, or access tokens.

Verification and Response Time

21Shift SpA may verify your identity and authority before acting on a request, particularly where the request concerns an organization, Page, or ad account. Our target response period is 30 calendar days, subject to applicable law and permitted extensions.

Revoke Access in Meta Business Settings

Business administrators may independently remove access in Meta Business Settings. Depending on their configuration, they can:

  • remove assigned Pages or ad accounts from the relevant person, System User, or app assignment;
  • remove the System User or app assignment that has access to the affected assets; and
  • explicitly revoke relevant access tokens in Meta Business Settings.

Generating a new token is not a substitute for explicitly revoking an existing token. The current reporting module uses read-only Meta Marketing API access through ads_read; it cannot create, edit, pause, activate, or delete ads or related entities.

Facebook Login, If Enabled

Facebook Login is not part of the current reporting module. If Facebook Login is separately enabled and explicitly authorized in the future, a user may remove that app connection through Facebook Settings & Privacy, then Settings, Apps and Websites, by selecting the relevant app and choosing Remove. This is separate from business asset and token controls in Meta Business Settings.

What We Delete

After a valid request is verified, we delete or de-identify applicable cached API data, exports, message content, and reports containing personal data where required. Message content is relevant only if the optional Page Messaging module is separately implemented and explicitly authorized with separate credentials and controls.

Information We May Retain

We may retain aggregated or de-identified information and records needed for legal, contractual, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute purposes where permitted. Retained information will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Confirmation and Contact

We will confirm completion using the requester's verified contact channel. For questions about this process, contact info@21shift.com.

Questions? Contact info@21shift.com.
