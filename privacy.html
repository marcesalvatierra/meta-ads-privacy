Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This policy explains how Stark Paid Media handles information in connection with authorized paid-media analysis and reporting.

Who We Are

Stark Paid Media is operated by 21Shift SpA under the 21Shift brand in Chile.

Scope of This Policy

Stark Paid Media supports authorized paid-media analysis and reporting for 21Shift and its clients. This policy applies to information processed through the application and its current integrations.

Information We Process

Advertising data may include account metadata; campaign, ad set, and ad names and status; budgets; targeting configuration; creative metadata; delivery information; and performance metrics. We process business or Page metadata only when the corresponding permission and asset access have been granted.

How We Use Information

We use information for reporting, analysis, troubleshooting, security, client support, and separately authorized messaging operations. We do not sell Meta Platform Data or use it for unrelated advertising.

Read-Only Paid Media Reporting Module

The current reporting integration uses read-only Meta Marketing API access through ads_read. It can retrieve authorized advertising information for analysis and reporting, but cannot create, edit, pause, activate, or delete ads or related entities.

Optional Page Messaging Module

The Optional Page Messaging Module is inactive unless it is separately implemented and explicitly authorized. If enabled, it may process message content, sender identifiers, timestamps, attachments, Page or Instagram account metadata, and authorized replies. Any such module requires separate credentials and controls.

Service Providers and Disclosures

Information may be processed by Meta, OpenAI/Codex, and infrastructure or professional service providers necessary to operate the service. These legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request logs. We may disclose information where necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, protect rights and security, or address disputes.

Data Storage and Retention

Raw API responses are generally transient but may be temporarily retained for troubleshooting or reproducibility. Derived reports may be stored locally for client service and business records. We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the client relationship, contractual obligations, legal compliance, security, or disputes.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

International Processing

This international processing may occur outside the requester's country where our service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

Your Choices and Rights
